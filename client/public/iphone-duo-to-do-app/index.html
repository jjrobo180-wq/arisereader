Why the iPhone Duo needs a different kind of to-do app

The iPhone Duo opens into a 7.6-inch screen with a fold down the middle, and closes into a 5.4-inch phone. A to-do app made for a regular iPhone simply stretches across that big screen: one long list, with the crease running right through it.

Arise To Duo treats the two halves as two places. Your lists live on one side, the tasks in the list you picked on the other, and tapping a task opens it without your list ever disappearing.

Questions people ask

What is the best to-do app for the iPhone Duo?

Arise To Duo is a to-do app designed around the iPhone Duo's fold. Opened flat, your menu and lists sit on one half of the 7.6-inch screen and your tasks on the other, so nothing important lands on the crease. Folded, it works like a normal phone app on the 5.4-inch outer screen. It is free to start.

Is Arise To Duo in the App Store?

Not yet. Today it runs as a web app: open arisetodo.com in Safari, sign in, then use Share › Add to Home Screen and it opens like an app. A native iPhone app is on the way.

Does the Duo layout turn on by itself?

Yes. On the iPhone Duo's inner screen Arise To Duo switches to its two-pane layout automatically. You can also set it to always on, and one switch in the Duo menu turns it off again, along with any split screen you have open.

Does it work when the iPhone Duo is folded?

Yes. On the 5.4-inch outer screen it uses the regular phone layout, and everything you added on the inner screen is already there.

Can I use split screen in Arise To Duo?

Yes. Arise To Duo has its own split screen, so you can put your to-do list beside your notes, or your grocery list beside the meal planner (teachers can add their Work hub too). It works on the Duo and on regular phones.

Is Arise To Duo free?

Yes. The free plan includes both hubs, tasks, lists, calendars, notes, chores and up to three apps from the App Store inside Arise. Unlimited is $5 a month for all 575+ apps and the AI features.

Does it sync between my iPhone Duo and other devices?

Yes. Your lists are saved to your account and show up on any phone, tablet or computer where you sign in.
