FAQ

Good to know.

Is Cooked Fitness by Arise free?

Yes. Workouts, programs, food logging with barcode scanning, sleep, runs, the period and medicine trackers and Find a trainer are all free, for as long as you like. Premium adds the AI and camera features and is part of Arise All Access, $10 a month.

What does Premium add?

Photo scanning for machines and home gyms, camera rep counting, an after-workout report, gyms near you with live crowd reports, healthier picks when you eat out, AI recipes, Sleep Lab, Ask Fit, the Pro+ layout and a free guest pass for one friend.

How does the guest pass work?

With Premium you get one guest pass. Send it to a friend or family member and they get Premium free on their own account for as long as you keep yours. You can switch who has it once every 30 days.

Does it work with Apple Health and Apple Watch?

Yes. Connect Apple Health and your sleep, steps, heart rate and workouts come in on their own. The Apple Watch app lets you log sets and see your rest timer.

Can I work with a real trainer?

Yes. Open Find a trainer, answer six quick questions and see your best three matches with their prices. Your trainer builds your plan in Cooked PT and sees only the workouts, food and health data you choose to share. You can stop sharing any time.

Can I use it without a gym?

Yes. Tell it you train at home and it builds workouts from what you have, even if that's just your bodyweight.

Is my health data private?

Your data is yours. It's never sold, and nothing goes to a trainer unless you choose to share it.

Do I need a new account?

No. If you already use Arise To Duo or Arise Notes, sign in with the same account.
