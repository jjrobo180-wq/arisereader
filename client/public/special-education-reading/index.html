INCLUSIVE READING

Reading activities for students with different learning needs.

A.R.I.S.E. Reader brings comprehension practice and visual learning together. Educators and families can choose book quizzes, picture-based activities, and accessible experiences that fit a student's interests and learning needs.

Explore book quizzes →See family tools

Practice understanding stories

Book quizzes can check characters, events, and main ideas after reading. Teachers can also build questions for classroom texts and lessons.

Learn with pictures and choices

Eye Gazer activities include visual learning games and customizable communication options for students who benefit from picture-based interactions.

Celebrate reading steps

Reading points, achievements, and optional games can help students see their progress and stay motivated without making learning only about a score.

Connect home and school

Parents and teachers can guide activities, celebrate milestones, and use available progress tools to support students.

How it works

  1. Choose a book, comprehension quiz, or visual activity that matches a student's learning goals.
  2. Give the student an accessible way to read, respond, or practice with support.
  3. Review the activity together and choose what to practice next.

Frequently asked questions

Is this only for special education students?

No. A.R.I.S.E. Reader supports a range of readers, including students who may benefit from additional visual or communication-based options.

Does the platform replace an IEP?

No. Teachers can select activities that align with classroom goals, but A.R.I.S.E. Reader does not replace an IEP, specialized instruction, or official progress-monitoring records.

Can families use it at home?

Yes. Family tools connect reading participation, rewards, and guided activities. Read more on the parent page.

Start with a story. Help every learner take the next step.

A.R.I.S.E. Reader helps students, teachers and families make reading engaging and track progress. Features and available plans may change.
