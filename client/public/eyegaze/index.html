Two ways to choose

However they reach the screen, it works.

Each learner gets the access style that fits them, and a grown-up can change it any time.

👀

Look and wait

Look at a button and keep your eyes there. A ring fills up, and the button is chosen. No clicking needed.

  • Choose how long to look before a button is picked
  • For eye-gaze devices that move the pointer on screen
👆

Tap or click

Choose a button with a finger, a switch, a mouse or a touch screen. Same big buttons, same calm layout.

  • Works on tablets, Chromebooks and computers
  • Switch to Look and wait whenever it helps

Getting started

Ready in three steps.

Make the account

Create an account with the Eye Gazer option turned on. Students in a class can join their teacher the same way.

Pick how they choose

Turn on Look and wait for an eye-gaze device, or keep Tap or click for touch, mouse or switch.

Make it theirs

Add their photos to My World, record family words in My Talker, and set what Shorts can show.

Questions

Good to know

Who is A.R.I.S.E. Eye Gazer for?

Learners who are non-verbal, use eye gaze, or do best with large, simple choices. It is a separate, calmer home screen inside A.R.I.S.E. Reader, so they can still read the same books as their class.

Do we need special equipment?

No. Eye Gazer works with a finger on a touch screen, a mouse, or a switch. For Look and wait, use an eye-gaze device that moves the pointer on screen.

Can a teacher use it with their students?

Yes. An Eye Gazer student can sign up with their school and teacher and join the class like any other student. See A.R.I.S.E. for teachers.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Take the tour. It needs no account, and nothing is saved.

What does it cost?

Eye Gazer is part of A.R.I.S.E. Reader, which is free for students and for parents with up to 3 kids. See the plans for everything else.
