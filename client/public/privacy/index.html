Arise To Duo

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026. This policy covers the Arise To Duo iPhone app, arisetodo.com and arisereader.com (Arise LifeHub, Arise WorkHub and A.R.I.S.E. Reader).

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseSupport
The short version. We collect what you put into Arise so it can be saved and synced for you. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use any tracking or advertising SDKs. Apple Health and Calendar data are read only when you turn them on, are used only to show in your own diary and calendar, and are never used for advertising or shared for marketing. You can delete your account and data in the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, username, email address, role (personal, parent, teacher or student), school and grade if you give them, and your password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Sign in with Apple (if you use it): Apple tells us an ID for your account, your name if you share it, and your email address, which can be Apple's private relay address. We use them only to sign you in. Deleting your account also unlinks it from your Apple ID.
  • What you add: tasks, lists, notes, calendar events, family and household information, goals and journal entries, files you upload, and messages you send in shared spaces. Teachers may add student information for their own classes (such as names, services, meeting notes and IEP planning details); that information is kept for the teacher and their school and is never sold or used for anything else.
  • Apple Health (iPhone app, only if you connect it): steps, active calories and body weight, read-only, to fill in your Food & fitness diary. Arise never writes to Apple Health. You can turn access off at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • iPhone Calendar (iPhone app, only if you connect it): the events from the calendars on your iPhone for the weeks around today, so they show on your Arise calendar. You can disconnect it in the app or turn access off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.
  • Calendar links you paste: the events in that calendar feed.
  • Reading activity (A.R.I.S.E. Reader): books read, quiz answers and scores, and points.
  • Technical information: basic logs our servers keep to run the service and stop abuse (such as IP address, device type and error reports). We don't use these to track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run Arise: save and sync your information, show it on your devices and to the people you share with.
  • To send emails you ask for or need (sign-in codes, password resets, invitations, reminders you set).
  • For AI features you choose to use (such as Add with AI or the IEP writer): the text you send is processed by our AI providers to give you an answer. It isn't used to advertise to you.
  • To keep Arise safe and fix problems.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run Arise for us, under contracts that limit their use of it: hosting (Render), database and file storage (Supabase), email delivery (Resend), text messages you ask us to send (Twilio), and AI processing for AI features (such as OpenAI, Groq and Perplexity). We may also disclose information if the law requires it. Apple Health data is never shared with third parties for advertising, marketing or data brokering.

Students and children

Student accounts are created by schools and teachers for classroom reading. We use student information only to provide Arise to the school, follow the school's instructions, and don't use it for advertising. Parents can ask to review or delete their child's information through the child's school or by contacting us. Arise To Duo's personal LifeHub and WorkHub accounts are for adults.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time: in the app, open the More menu (⋯) → Account & privacy → Delete my account. This removes your account and the information that belongs to it. Copies in our backups are removed within 30 days. You can also ask us to delete it from the Support page.

Your choices

  • See, change or export what you've added at any time in the app.
  • Turn off Apple Health, Calendar and notification access in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information. Contact us and we'll help.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and access to our database is restricted. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and for important changes we'll tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the form on our Support page.
