Stop switching between your notes and your list

Meeting notes in one app, the to-do list in another: every time you flip between them you lose your place. Arise To Duo keeps both in one app and can show them together, so the notes stay in view while you turn them into tasks.

How to put your notes beside your list

  1. Open CustomizeIn Arise To Duo, tap Customize (on a phone it's in the ⋯ menu).
  2. Choose “Duo & split screen”Set one half to To-Do list and the other to Notes.
  3. Tap Open split screenYour list and notes open together. Swap sides or change either one from the handle in the middle.

Questions people ask

Is there a to-do list app with notes in the same place?

Yes. Arise To Duo has tasks and notes in one app, and its split screen shows both at once: your to-do list on one side or half of the screen and your notes on the other.

Can I see my notes and tasks at the same time on an iPhone?

Yes. Regular iPhones can't run two apps side by side, but Arise To Duo's built-in split screen puts your to-do list and notes on screen together, one above the other.

Can I turn my notes into tasks?

You can add tasks right beside your notes in split screen. With Unlimited, you can also paste notes or a photo and the AI turns them into tasks and events.

Are there note templates?

Yes. Notes come with templates to start from, and you can add more note apps from the App Store inside Arise.

Is it free?

Yes. Tasks, notes and split screen are all in the free plan. Unlimited is $5 a month for every app and the AI features.
