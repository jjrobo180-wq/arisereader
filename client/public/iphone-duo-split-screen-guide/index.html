iPhone Duo guide

How split screen works on the iPhone Duo

Apple's first foldable can run two apps side by side on its 7.6-inch inner screen. Here's what split screen on the iPhone Duo can do, and how to keep your to-do list right beside it.

The iPhone Duo at a glance

7.6-inch inner screen

Opens like a book, with the fold down the middle. This is where split screen happens.

5.4-inch outer screen

A regular-size phone when it's closed.

iOS 27, adapted for the fold

Controls move to the side, the Home Screen docks vertically, and apps can run in pairs.

On sale October 23, 2026

Preorders opened October 16. Apple lists it from $1,999 with 256GB.

What you can do with split screen on the Duo

  • Two apps side by side. Move one app aside and open another next to it.
  • Two windows of one app. For example, two Safari windows to compare things.
  • Swap and rearrange. Gestures swap the apps or move them to the other side.
  • Saved app pairs. Tap the divider to keep a pair you use often.
  • Video on top. Held upright, a video can stay pinned above another app.
  • Half-folded use. Partly fold the phone and content moves away from the crease.

Apple's own support pages have the exact gestures for each step.

The best split screen for your to-do list

Pairing your to-do app with your notes app works, but it uses both halves for two separate apps. Arise To Duo puts that pairing inside one app: lists on one half and tasks on the other when you simply open it, or any two of its views with its built-in split screen. That leaves Apple's split screen free for something else, like a video call next to your plan for the day.

Questions people ask

Does the iPhone Duo have split screen?

Yes. Apple adapted iOS 27 for the iPhone Duo so you can run two apps side by side on the 7.6-inch inner screen and save app pairs to come back to later.

How big are the iPhone Duo's screens?

The inner screen is 7.6 inches and the outer screen is 5.4 inches.

When did the iPhone Duo come out?

Apple announced the iPhone Duo on September 9, 2026. Preorders opened October 16 and it went on sale October 23, 2026.

Can I open two windows of the same app?

Yes. Apple showed two Safari windows side by side, for example to compare products.

Can I watch a video while using another app?

Yes. Held upright, a video can be pinned to the top half while you use another app below.

Which apps work best in split screen on the iPhone Duo?

Apple optimized its own apps, and new developer tools let other apps build Duo layouts; Apple showed Zoom and Slack using them. For to-dos, Arise To Duo is built around the fold and has its own split screen inside the app.

Do I need split screen to see my tasks and notes together?

Not in Arise To Duo. Its built-in split screen puts your to-do list beside your notes, calendar or grocery list in one app, on the Duo or on any other phone.
