How it works

  1. Sign in with a teacher account and open the Teacher Workspace.
  2. Set up students, meetings, and tasks relevant to your work.
  3. Use it as an organizational aid alongside your district's approved records and privacy requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Who is this workspace for?

The workspace supports educators who manage student-related planning, including special education teachers and case managers.

Does it replace my district's IEP software?

No. It is an organization and planning aid, not a replacement for official IEP records, district systems, or legal compliance procedures.

Can I use reading quizzes with my class too?

Yes. The teacher tools include book quizzes, class activities, and reading progress features.
