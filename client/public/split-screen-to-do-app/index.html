How to open split screen

  1. Open CustomizeIn Arise To Duo, tap Customize (on a phone it's in the ⋯ menu).
  2. Choose “Duo & split screen”Pick what goes in the top and bottom halves. A small picture shows how it will look on your screen.
  3. Tap Open split screenBoth halves open at once. Use the handle in the middle to swap sides, change a view or close the split.

One floating add button serves both halves: drag it to the side you want, then tap to add a task, an event, a note and more, or ask the AI assistant.

Questions people ask

Can you use split screen on a regular iPhone?

Regular iPhones don't let you run two apps side by side; Apple added app split screen with the foldable iPhone Duo. Arise To Duo gets around that inside the app: its own split screen shows two of its views at once on any iPhone, one above the other.

How do I open split screen in Arise To Duo?

Open Customize, choose the Duo & split screen tab, pick what goes in each half, then tap Open split screen. On the iPhone Duo you can also open it from the Duo button in the top bar.

What can I put on each side?

Any two of: your to-do list, notes, calendar, grocery list, meal planner, chores, goals, habits, journal, money, countdowns, focus timer, browser or your Life hub home. Teachers can also put their Work hub on one side.

Does split screen work on Android phones and iPads?

Yes. It works the same in any modern browser. On a phone held upright the halves sit top and bottom; on a tablet, a computer or a phone turned sideways they sit side by side.

Do changes on one side show up on the other?

Yes. Each half saves to your account, and when one side saves, the other catches up straight away.

How do I close split screen?

Tap the handle between the two halves and choose Close split screen. On the iPhone Duo, switching Arise To Duo off in the Duo menu closes it too.

Is split screen free?

Yes. Split screen is part of the free plan.
