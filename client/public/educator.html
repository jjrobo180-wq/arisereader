Questions

Good questions.

Is it really free for teachers?

Yes. The Work and Life hubs are free for teachers, specialists and parents, with no trial that runs out. Arise All Access is an optional $10 a month for every plugin and AI.

Is Arise Reader free?

Yes, right now Arise Reader is free for every student, parent and teacher.

Does it work on a computer, iPad and phone?

It works in any web browser, and Arise To Duo is also an iPhone app. Everything stays in step between them.

Can my whole school use it?

Yes. Teachers can start on their own today. If you want to bring a whole school or district on, get in touch.

Is the iPhone Duo version still here?

Yes. Arise To Duo was built for the iPhone Duo's two screens, and that's all on the iPhone Duo page.
