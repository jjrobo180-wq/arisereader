READ • LEARN • EARN

A reading incentive program built around real books.

Reading rewards work best when they keep the book at the center. In A.R.I.S.E. Reader, students complete reading quizzes to earn points and recognition, with optional games and virtual rewards to celebrate their effort.

Earn points by reading

Students take quizzes about what they've read. Their results help determine points, achievements and progress toward reading goals.

Celebrate each success

Certificates, leaderboards and reading milestones create moments for teachers and families to recognize effort and growing reading habits.

Connect screen time to learning

In-app games and customization give students something to work toward. Teachers and parents can manage play time so reading stays the priority.

Make reading feel social

Students can participate in classroom activities and reading competitions that encourage discussion and a shared love of stories.

The reward is a reason to begin. The book is why they grow.

A.R.I.S.E. Reader helps students, teachers and families make reading engaging and track progress. Features and available plans may change.
