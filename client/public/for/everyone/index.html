Questions

Questions from people like you, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. LifeHub is free to start. Arise All Access ($10 a month) adds every plugin and the AI features.

Can my family share it?

Yes. FamilyHub gives your family a shared calendar, chores and lists.

Does it work with my calendar?

Yes. Connect Google Calendar or your iPhone calendar, or add any calendar link.

Does it sync across my devices?

Yes. Everything is saved to your account, so the same lists are on your phone, tablet and computer.

Can I hide the tools I don't need?

Yes. Turn on the tools you use, hide the rest, and add more from the Plugin Gallery any time.
