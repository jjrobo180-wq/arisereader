Questions

Questions from stylists and barbers, answered.

Can I save color formulas?

Yes. Each client card holds formulas and notes.

Does it book appointments?

Find a Time lets clients pick a time that works for you both.

What does it cost?

WorkHub is free to start. Arise All Access ($10 a month) adds every plugin and the AI features.

Does it sync across my devices?

Yes. Everything is saved to your account, so the same lists are on your phone, tablet and computer.

Can I hide the tools I don't need?

Yes. Turn on the tools you use, hide the rest, and add more from the Plugin Gallery any time.
