BOOK QUIZZES & COMPREHENSION

Free reading comprehension quizzes that make books worth finishing.

A.R.I.S.E. Reader helps students turn their reading into an activity they can finish, discuss, and celebrate. Students take book quizzes, see their scores, and collect points as they work through more stories. It's free for students and families.

Read, then show what you understood

Reading comprehension grows with practice. A.R.I.S.E. pairs books with questions about what happened, who the characters are, and what readers can learn from the text. Teachers can also build quizzes for the books and lessons they use.

Make progress visible

After a quiz, students can see points, achievements, and reading activity. Teachers can check quiz results and follow reading progress without relying only on a paper reading log.

Quizzes for the books they pick

If a book doesn't have a quiz yet, students can ask for one. Requests are checked against real books and approved before the quiz goes live, and students hear when it's ready.

Designed for different learners

Read-aloud and eye-gaze-friendly quiz modes are built in, and A.R.I.S.E. Eye Gazer gives non-verbal learners a calm screen of big choices, so every student can show what they understood.

How a book quiz works

  1. Finish a bookStudents pick from the library, or read a book of their own choosing.
  2. Take the quizA short set of comprehension questions checks what they understood. Each book's quiz can be taken once, so scores stay fair.
  3. Earn and celebratePoints go toward levels, streaks, the leaderboard and printable certificates, plus Reader Coins to spend in the game worlds.

For students, families and teachers

  • Students read, take quizzes and watch their progress grow.
  • Parents link up to three kids for free and follow their reading, certificates and game time.
  • Teachers see every student's quiz results, build custom quizzes and host live class quiz games.

Questions teachers and parents ask

Are the reading quizzes free?

Yes. Book quizzes in A.R.I.S.E. Reader are free for every student and for parents with up to 3 linked children.

What grade levels are the quizzes for?

A.R.I.S.E. Reader is built for elementary and middle school readers, with books and quizzes across a range of reading levels.

What kind of questions are in a book quiz?

Questions check understanding of what happened, who the characters are and what readers can learn from the text.

What if my child's book doesn't have a quiz yet?

Students can ask for a quiz for the book they read. The request is checked against real books and approved before the quiz goes live.

Can teachers make their own quizzes?

Yes. Teachers can build quizzes for the books and lessons they use, and run live, code-based quiz games with the whole class.

Can students who don't read aloud or use their hands take the quizzes?

Yes. Quizzes have read-aloud and eye-gaze-friendly modes, and A.R.I.S.E. Eye Gazer lets students answer by looking or tapping.
