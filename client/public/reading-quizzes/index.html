BOOK QUIZZES & COMPREHENSION

Reading comprehension quizzes that make books worth finishing.

A.R.I.S.E. Reader helps students turn their reading into an activity they can finish, discuss, and celebrate. Students take book quizzes, see their scores, and collect points as they work through more stories.

Read, then show what you understood

Reading comprehension grows with practice. A.R.I.S.E. pairs books with questions about what happened, who the characters are, and what readers can learn from the text. Teachers can also build quizzes for the books and lessons they use.

Make progress visible

After a quiz, students can see points, achievements, and reading activity. Teachers can check quiz results and follow reading progress without relying only on a paper reading log.

Designed for different learners

A.R.I.S.E. includes accessible learning experiences alongside traditional reading and quiz tools. Students can participate in ways that match their learning needs, while families and educators help guide their experience.
