CLASSROOM READING TOOLS

Reading quizzes, classroom games, and progress tools for teachers.

Bring books, comprehension questions and student motivation into one place. A.R.I.S.E. Reader helps teachers assign or create quizzes, see reading activity, and use learning games as an incentive—not a distraction.

Create a teacher account →Visit A.R.I.S.E. Reader

Book quizzes and live classroom review

Use comprehension quizzes to check understanding after reading. Create custom questions for books or lessons, and host live, code-based classroom games to review topics together.

Track readers without chasing logs

View quiz attempts, scores and points in student profiles. Celebrate reading milestones with certificates and class recognition while identifying students who may need another check-in.

Stay in control of game time

Teacher controls can limit play, set closing hours, and connect game access to academic activities. This helps keep the focus on reading and learning.

Support diverse learners

A.R.I.S.E. includes accessibility-focused options and family connections, so teachers can tailor the experience for students with different learning needs.

Make reading practice something your students want to come back to.

A.R.I.S.E. Reader helps students, teachers and families make reading engaging and track progress. Features and available plans may change.
