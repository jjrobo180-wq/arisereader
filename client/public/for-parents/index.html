READING TOGETHER AT HOME

Help your child feel proud of every book they finish.

A.R.I.S.E. Reader helps families connect reading practice with progress kids can see. A linked parent account lets you celebrate achievements, review quiz activity, and guide the games and rewards your child can access.

Create a parent account →Visit A.R.I.S.E. Reader

See what your child is reading

Review completed book quizzes, scores, and points in the family portal. You'll have a clearer starting point for talking about stories, characters, and what your child learned.

Turn achievements into motivation

Quizzes connect to reading points, certificates, and in-app rewards. The goal is to help children look forward to their next book, not just finish an assignment.

Guide the experience

Parents can set game-time controls and use a family proctor code to support supervised quizzes at home. Families can connect with school-based reading activity when their child's account is linked.

Options for different learning needs

Accessibility-focused experiences, including visual communication and learning activities, support families whose children benefit from different ways of engaging.

A little reading progress is worth celebrating.
