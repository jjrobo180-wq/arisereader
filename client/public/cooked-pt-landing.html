FAQ

Good to know.

How much does Cooked PT cost?

$29 a month after a 14-day free trial, with unlimited clients. Cancel any time. When clients pay you by card in the app, Arise keeps 1%. With your own payment links, you keep everything.

Do my clients have to pay for an app?

No. Your clients use Cooked Fitness by Arise for free. They can add Premium through Arise All Access if they want it, but they don't need it to work with you.

What can I see about my clients?

Only what each client chooses to share: workouts, food, sleep, weight and other health data from Cooked Fitness by Arise. They can change it or stop sharing any time.

Can I bring my clients from another app?

Yes. Import from Trainerize, TrueCoach or a CSV file. You can export your data any time, too.

How do form checks work?

Your client films a set in Cooked and sends it to you. You pause on any frame, draw on it and add a voice note. It's saved to their history so you can compare over time.

How do I get new clients?

You get a spot in the Arise trainer directory and a trainer website. Cooked Fitness by Arise members answer six quick questions and see their best three matches, so the right people find you.

Do I get my own app?

Yes. Your branded app has your name, colors and logo at your own link. It's included while Cooked PT is in early access.

Does it work in person and online?

Both. Coach in person, remotely or both, and run groups from the same place.
