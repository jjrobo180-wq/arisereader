Chime of Aoren

The sky islands of Aoren stay aloft because their great bells never stop ringing. Tonight one of them went silent, and the only person who noticed was a bell-mender's apprentice who has never heard a Tone in her life.

How Resonance works

In Aoren, sound is power. A person who can hear a Chime's voice is a Tuner, and these are the rules every Tuner lives by.

  • A Tone costs Breath. Ember and Gale drain the blue bar. Run dry and the Tone will not answer.
  • Every strike is a note. Landing hits with the mallet gives Breath back, so you earn your Tones by fighting.
  • Two Tones make a Chord. Played together they are stronger than either alone. A Chord fills as you fight.
  • Silence is not empty. The Hushed are holes where sound used to be. They cannot be reasoned with, only rung away.
