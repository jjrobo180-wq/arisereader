Tutorial

How to use Arise Notes.

From your first recording to your to-dos in about two minutes.

1

Make your free account

  1. Tap Create a free account.
  2. Use your Arise account if you already have one.
  3. On a phone, add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
2

Record a meeting

  1. On the Notes page, tap Record a meeting.
  2. Pick a template, like Staff meeting or IEP meeting.
  3. Keep typing your own notes while it records.
3

Or upload a file

  1. Tap Upload on the Notes page.
  2. Choose an audio or video file up to 2 hours.
  3. The transcript and summary appear when it's done.
4

Use the commands

  1. Type @ariserecord in any note to start.
  2. @arisepause, @ariseresume and @arisemark while you talk.
  3. @arisestop writes the summary into the note.
5

Send your to-dos

  1. Open the To-dos part of the note.
  2. Check who's doing what and when.
  3. Send them to your Arise To Duo tasks and calendar.
6

Ask and share

  1. Tap Ask to ask about one note, all notes or anything.
  2. Share a view-only link or export to PDF or Word.
  3. Add more tools from the App Gallery.

Questions

Arise Notes, answered.

What is Arise Notes?

Arise Notes is a notes super app. It records or takes an uploaded file and turns it into a transcript, a summary and to-dos, and it's also a full place to write your own notes, ask AI questions and use extra apps like Sticky Notes, Whiteboard and Flashcards.

Is Arise Notes free?

Yes. The free plan has every notes tool and 60 AI minutes a month. Arise All Access is $10 a month for every Arise app with AI, including Arise Notes Plus with 1,500 minutes a month.

What's an AI minute?

One minute of audio that Arise Notes transcribes and summarizes. Typing notes doesn't use minutes.

How do I record inside a note?

Tap Record a meeting, tap the mic in any note, or type @ariserecord. Use @arisepause, @ariseresume, @arisemark and @arisestop while you record. When you stop, the summary is added to the same note.

Does it work with Arise To Duo?

Yes. It's the same account. To-dos and dates from your notes can go straight into your Arise To Duo tasks and calendar.

Can I share a note with someone who doesn't have an account?

Yes. Send a view-only link and anyone can open it. You can also export notes to PDF or Word.

What is Undercover?

An AI chat in the App Gallery that looks like an ordinary app, like a text thread, a group chat or a document, so you can use AI quietly in a meeting.
