Questions people ask

What is a good to-do app for families?

One that keeps the whole household in one place. Arise To Duo has a family calendar, a chore chart with rotations and points, a shared grocery list and a meal planner, and you can assign tasks to each person in the family.

Can I assign chores and tasks to my kids?

Yes. Add each family member, then assign tasks to them. The chore chart shows who does what each day, with check-offs, rotations and points.

Can family members see the list without an account?

Yes. You can share a part of your family hub, like the grocery list or calendar, with a link anyone can open without an account. You can also send a quick family poll.

Does it have reminders?

Yes. Tasks can have due dates, times and repeats, and reminders can be sent to your phone.

Is it free for the whole family?

Yes. The free plan includes the family calendar, chores, grocery list, notes and up to three apps from the App Store inside Arise. Unlimited is $5 a month for all 575+ apps and the AI features.

Does it work on our different phones?

Yes. It runs on iPhone, Android, tablets and computers, and everything is saved to your account.
