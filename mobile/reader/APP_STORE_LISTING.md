## Description (draft)

Make reading an adventure with A.R.I.S.E. Reader.

A.R.I.S.E. Reader brings reading quizzes, comprehension practice and encouraging rewards together in one place. Students can explore books, answer reading questions and follow their progress. Families and teachers can support their reading journeys with tools made for different learners.

Features on the existing platform include:
- Book quizzes and comprehension activities
- Reading points, rewards and progress tracking
- Student, parent and educator experiences
- Interactive learning games and activities
- Accessible reading and communication features for learners who need additional support

Feature availability may depend on account type, school settings and device. The iOS version must be tested before these features are promised in a published listing.
