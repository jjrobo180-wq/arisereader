A foldable's big screen deserves more than a stretched list

Book-style foldables open into a near-square screen with a crease down the middle. Most to-do apps just widen their phone layout to fill it, so you get one long list with lots of empty space and a fold running through it.

Arise To Duo uses the two halves as two places: lists on one side, the tasks in the list you picked on the other. Close the phone and it goes back to a normal phone layout on the cover screen, with everything already saved.

Questions people ask

What is the best to-do app for a foldable phone?

Look for one that uses both halves of the big inner screen instead of stretching a phone layout across the fold. Arise To Duo shows your lists on one half and your tasks on the other, works on the cover screen when folded, and has its own split screen.

Does Arise To Duo work on the Galaxy Z Fold and Pixel Fold?

Yes. On book-style foldables such as the iPhone Duo, Galaxy Z Fold and Pixel Fold it switches to its two-pane layout when you open the phone. If your phone doesn't switch on its own, open the Duo menu and choose to use the layout always.

What about flip phones like the Galaxy Z Flip or Razr?

Flip phones open into a regular tall phone screen, so Arise To Duo uses its normal phone layout there, and its split screen shows two views one above the other.

How do I install it on an Android foldable?

Open arisetodo.com in Chrome, sign in, then choose Add to Home screen (or Install app) from the menu. It opens full screen like any other app.

Can I use it next to another app in my phone's own split screen?

Yes. Arise To Duo runs in your browser, so you can put the browser next to another app using your phone's own multi-window feature, or use Arise To Duo's built-in split screen inside the app.
