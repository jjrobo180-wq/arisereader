Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026. This policy covers the Pod+ iPhone app and the Pod+ website.

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseSupport
The short version. You can listen without an account. We keep what's needed to run Pod+: your listening preferences, and if you use Pod+ Social, what you post. We don't sell your data and we don't use tracking or advertising SDKs. You can delete your data in the app at any time.

What we collect

  • A random device ID made by the app, so your likes, follows and profile stay yours without an account.
  • Your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash) if you sign in or become a Pod+ Member.
  • Sign in with Apple (if you use it): Apple tells us an ID for your account, your name if you share it, and your email address, which can be Apple's private relay address. We use them only to sign you in. Deleting your account also unlinks it from your Apple ID.
  • Listening information: the shows you follow, your interests and progress (most of it is kept only on your device), how long creators' episodes are played (for creators' stats), and ad-skip votes.
  • Study Pod+ and Meeting Pod: the notes you paste or add are sent to our AI provider to write your episode or summary, and are not saved on our servers or shown to anyone else. What's made is kept on your device. The audio is kept for a short time so it plays again quickly.
  • Pod+ Social: your profile (name, username, bio, photo), posts, comments, clips, stories, direct messages, photos and videos you upload, episodes you publish, likes, follows, blocks and reports.
  • Technical information: basic logs to run the service and stop abuse (such as IP address and screen size). We don't use these to track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To play podcasts, audiobooks and music, recommend shows, and keep your place.
  • To run Pod+ Social and show your posts to others. Your profile and posts are public; direct messages are seen only by the people in the conversation, and by us if a message is reported.
  • To keep Pod+ safe: photos and text are checked automatically for objectionable content (by our moderation provider) before they're posted, and reports are reviewed by us.
  • For AI features you choose (transcripts, Ask AI): the episode's audio or text is processed by our AI providers.
  • To send sign-in codes and messages you ask for.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run Pod+ for us: hosting (Render), database and file storage (Supabase), email (Resend), and moderation and AI processing (such as OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram and Perplexity). Podcast listings come from Apple Podcasts' public directory, audiobooks from LibriVox and music from the Internet Archive; when you play something, its audio is fetched from the podcast's host. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Children

Pod+ Social is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information until you delete it. In the app, open the menu → Account & privacy → Delete my account (or Delete my data if you don't have an account). This removes your account, profile, posts, comments, messages, stories, clips, uploaded episodes, follows and likes. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days. You can also ask from the Support page.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed and access to our database is restricted.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app about important changes.
