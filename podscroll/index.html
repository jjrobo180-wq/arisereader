Questions

Good to know

Is Pod+ free?

Yes. Listening, ad skip, Social and making your own show are all free to use.

Do I need an account?

Not to listen. What you pick, like, follow and save is kept in your browser. Make a quick profile when you want to post, clip, reply or publish.

Are these real podcasts?

Yes. Every show in the feed is a real podcast from Apple Podcasts, so the shows you already love are here too.

How does ad skip work?

Pod+ finds the ad breaks in each episode and jumps past them while you listen. If it skips something that wasn't an ad, tap Undo. If it misses one, mark it and Pod+ remembers.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, iPad or a computer. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
