Questions

Good to know

Is Pod+ free?

Yes. Listening, ad skip, Social and making your own show are all free to use.

Do I need an account?

Not to listen. What you pick, like, follow and save is kept in your browser. Make a quick profile when you want to post, clip, reply or publish.

Are these real podcasts?

Yes. Every show in the feed is a real podcast from Apple Podcasts, so the shows you already love are here too.

How does ad skip work?

Pod+ finds the ad breaks in each episode and jumps past them while you listen. If it skips something that wasn't an ad, tap Undo. If it misses one, mark it and Pod+ remembers.

I'm a new podcaster. How do people find me?

Publish an episode on Pod+ and it goes into Open Mic, where new and small creators are put first. Strong new episodes also show up inside For You. Followers get a notification every time you post, and you can go live with a LivePod any time.

Can I make money on Pod+?

Yes, starting soon. Pod+ Rise, the creator program, opens with supporter memberships, premium episodes and series, and Cheer payouts from your LivePods. Track your progress and join the waitlist in Studio today.

Do I get to set my own prices?

Yes. When Pod+ Rise opens, you choose your monthly supporter price and the price of any premium episode or series, and you decide which episodes stay free.

What can I do on the social side?

Post about what you're listening to, clip a moment and share it, reply, repost and quote, follow listeners and creators, swipe the Clips feed, and join LivePods to chat with hosts and send Cheers.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, iPad or a computer. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.
